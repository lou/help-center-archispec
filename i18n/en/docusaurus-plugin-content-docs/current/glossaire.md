# Archispec glossary

Definitions of terms used in Archispec and in this help center.

## A

**Agency** — Shared workspace grouping members, catalogue, and projects.

**Annotation** — Comment anchored to a specific spot on an image or PDF, with an internal or external discussion thread. See [Annotations](/projet/annotations).

**Administrator** — Intermediate role at agency or project level. Can manage members and settings, but cannot transfer ownership or archive a project.

**Associate** — Base role for team members. Can work on products, services, files, sections, lots, 3D scene, and chat.

**Archiving** — Action that removes an item (project or product) from the active list without deleting it. Archiving is reversible.

**Progress** — Name of the planning tab on the client side (equivalent to **Planning** on the agency side).

## B

**Budget** — Project tab (admins / owners) to track envelopes and consumption. See [Budget](/projet/budget).

**Purchase order** — PDF export to order products from a supplier. See [Purchase order](/produits/bon-de-commande).

## C

**Catalogue** — Agency's central product library, reusable across all projects. See [Catalogue](/catalogue).

**Client** — External person invited to a project to review proposed products, validate choices, and exchange via chat.

**External channel** — Chat or annotation thread visible to the team and project clients.

**3D credit** — Unit consumed to generate a 3D model from a product image (1 credit per generation).

**3D scene** — Interactive project model (one per project), published from SketchUp or imported as `.glb` / `.gltf`, viewed in Orbit or Walk mode. See [Project 3D scene](/projet/scene-3d).

## D

**Detail booklet** — PDF export grouping selected product sheets.

**Draft** — Initial product status, still hidden from the client. See [Product statuses](/produits/statuts-produits).

## E

**Envelope** — Budget amount allocated to the project or to a lot. See [Budget](/projet/budget).

**Company** — Agency-level record of an intervening company, assignable as a lot contractor. See [Companies](/agence/entreprises).

## F

**File version** — New revision of a project file, with history and optional suspension of annotations on previous versions. See [Files](/projet/fichiers).

**Spreadsheet** — Excel export of selected products.

**Supply / install** — Product field (when a lot is defined) indicating whether the lot handles supply, installation, both, or neither.

## I

**In production** — Purchase status: the product is being manufactured / prepared. See [Purchases and tracking](/produits/achats-et-suivi).

**Internal channel** — Chat or annotation thread visible to the project team only.

## L

**Lot** — Trade category in a project (plumbing, carpentry, etc.). Products, services, and planning tasks can be linked to a lot. See [Lot management](/projet/gestion-lots).

## M

**Mention** — Notification sent to a member via `@` in chat or an annotation. See [Chat](/projet/chat).

## N

**Technical note** — Internal text field on a product, never visible to clients, exportable in the booklet and spreadsheet.

## O

**Owner** — Highest role. At agency or project level, has all permissions including archiving and ownership transfer.

## P

**Phase** — Grouping of planning tasks by project stage (Studies, Construction, etc.). See [Planning](/projet/planning).

**Planning** — Chronological view (Gantt-style) of project tasks, with phases, dependencies, validation deadlines, and client or external-link sharing.

**Planning share link** — Password-protected URL to view planning read-only without an account. See [Planning](/projet/planning).

**Client portal** — Dedicated interface (`/client/...`) accessible to invited clients on a project. See [Overview](/portail-client/vue-ensemble).

**Purchases** — Cross-project view of products being sourced and client validations to track. See [Purchases and tracking](/produits/achats-et-suivi).

**Product** — Central element of the specification: furniture, equipment, finish, or load. Each product has a status, price, images, and can be linked to a section and lot.

**Proposed** — Status indicating a product or service is presented to the client. Only proposed items (and beyond in the lifecycle) are visible on the client side.

**Referral** — Program allowing an agency to refer another agency and receive subscription credits. See [Referral](/facturation/parrainage).

## S

**Section** — Spatial division of a project (room, floor, space). Used to organize and filter products. See [Section management](/projet/gestion-sections).

**Seat** — User seat included in the paid subscription. Each active member or pending invitation consumes a seat.

**Service** — Service line (installation, labor, study, fees) that the client can validate, distinct from a product. See [Services](/produits/prestations).

**Status** — Stage in a product's lifecycle (proposed, validated, ordered, in production, etc.). See [Product statuses](/produits/statuts-produits).

## T

**Type** — Agency-level product category used for filtering and organizing. See [Product types](/agence/types-produits).

## V

**Validation deadline** — Due date for the client to validate products or services for a lot or item. See [Validation deadlines](/projet/echeances-validation).
