# Validate products (client side)

The client portal lets your clients share feedback on the products you propose, without accessing the full agency interface.

## Visible products

A client only sees products with **Proposed** status or later. **Unspecified** products remain invisible until the agency proposes them.

See [Product statuses](/produits/statuts-produits) for the full lifecycle.

## Client actions

On each proposed product, the client can:

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Like** | Product moves to **Liked** status |
| **Reject** | Product moves to **Rejected** status |
| **Approve** | Product moves to **Approved** status |

Approval requires confirmation before being saved.

The client can also undo their choice and return a product to **Proposed** status.

## Agency-only actions

Post-approval statuses are managed by the team:

- **Ordered**
- **In delivery**
- **Received**
- **Installed**

The agency updates these statuses as the build progresses.

## Notifications

The agency can be notified when:

- A client changes a product's status
- A client sends a message in chat

Configure your preferences in [Notifications](/projet/notifications).

## Best practices (agency side)

1. Make sure the product is complete (images, price, description) before moving it to **Proposed**
2. Use [client preview](/projet/previsualisation-client) to check the display
3. Adjust [sharing settings](/projet/parametres-partage) (prices, links) before inviting
4. Respond quickly to approvals via external chat

## Per-product chat

Each product has its own discussion thread. The client can ask questions about a specific product without leaving its page.

Project chat (the **Chat** tab) remains available for general communication.
