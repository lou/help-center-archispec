# Archive a product

Archiving removes a product from the active list without permanently deleting it.

## Archive a product individually

1. Open the product page
2. Click the **⋮** menu (actions)
3. Select **Archive**
4. The product disappears from the default list

## Archive multiple products

1. Select the products to archive (checkboxes)
2. Click **Archive** in the action bar
3. Confirm the action

See [Select products](/produits/selection) for bulk action details.

## Show archived products

1. Open the filter bar (filter icon at the top left)
2. Enable the **Archived products** switch

Only archived products are displayed. Disable the switch to return to the active list.

## Restore an archived product

1. Enable the **Archived products** filter
2. Open the product page or select it in the list
3. Click **Restore** (**⋮** menu or bulk action bar)

The product reappears in the active list.

## Effects of archiving

- The product and its files are preserved
- Product chat history is preserved
- The product no longer appears in the default list or in current exports
