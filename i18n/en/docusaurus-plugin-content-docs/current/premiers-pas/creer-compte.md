# Create your account

This guide walks you from sign-up to your first sign-in on Archispec.

## Sign up

1. Go to [archispec.fr](https://archispec.fr)
2. Click **Sign up**
3. Enter your email address and password
4. Submit the form

## Verify your email

1. Check your inbox
2. Open the verification email sent by Archispec
3. Click the confirmation link

Your account is activated once the email is verified.

## Accept the terms of use

On your first sign-in, you must accept the general terms of use to access the application.

## Sign in

1. Go to [archispec.fr/signin](https://archispec.fr/signin)
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **Sign in**
