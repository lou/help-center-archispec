# Set up your agency

Your agency is your team's shared workspace: projects, catalog, and members are grouped together.

## Create an agency

If you just signed up:

1. Enter your agency name
2. Confirm creation

Your agency is created and you are the owner.

## Join an existing agency

If a colleague invited you:

1. Open the invitation email
2. Click the link to join the agency
3. Sign in or create an account if needed

## Invite colleagues

1. In the main menu, click your agency name
2. Go to **Members**
3. Click **Invite a new member**
4. Enter the email and choose the role
5. Click **Send invitation**

For role details, see [Member management](/agence/gestion-membres) and [Roles and permissions](/comprendre/roles-permissions).

## Configure the agency

From the agency menu, you can also:

- Manage [product types](/agence/types-produits)
- Build the [companies](/agence/entreprises) contractor directory
- Manage manufacturers and suppliers
