# Area management

Areas let you organize your project by rooms, floors, or any other logical division.

## Create an area

1. Go to project **Settings**
2. Click **Areas** in the sidebar
3. Click **Add an area**
4. Enter the area name (e.g. "Ground floor", "Kitchen", "Bedroom 1")
5. Optionally, add a description
6. Click **Create**

## Edit an area

1. Go to project **Settings**
2. Click **Areas**
3. Click the area you want to edit
4. Edit the name or description
5. Click **Save**

## Reorder areas

You can reorder areas by drag and drop:

1. Go to project **Settings**
2. Click **Areas**
3. Click and hold an area
4. Drag it to the desired position
5. Release to confirm

## Delete an area

1. Go to project **Settings**
2. Click **Areas**
3. Click the **trash icon** to the right of the area
4. Confirm the action

**Warning**: If the area contains products, they will all be unassigned from the area and appear as "No area".
