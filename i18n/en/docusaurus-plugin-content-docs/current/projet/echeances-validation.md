# Validation deadlines

Set a due date for the client to validate a set of products or services. Deadlines appear on the [planning](/projet/planning) timeline and in the **To validate** / **Purchases** views.

## Create a deadline

You can attach a deadline:

- to a **lot** (all products / services in the lot without their own date)
- directly to a **product** or **service**

### From a lot

1. Open the **Lots** tab, or the lot panel
2. Add a **validation deadline**
3. Enter the **Validate by** date
4. Optionally give a **title** (e.g. “Ground floor tiling”)

### From planning

Lot deadlines also appear on the planning timeline. You can position them relative to the lot start (same day, X days before or after).

## Track validations on the agency side

1. Open **Purchases** or the validation tracking view
2. Filter on **Client validation**
3. Spot items that are **overdue**, due today, or upcoming

Each proposed product or service with a due date appears in these lists.

## Client side

The client has a **To validate** / **Choices to validate** queue that groups proposed items, with or without a deadline. Overdue items are highlighted.

See [Validate products](/portail-client/validation-produits).

## Reminders

In [notification preferences](/projet/notifications), enable **validation reminders** to receive a morning summary of overdue validations, and a reminder the day before a deadline.

## Delete a deadline

Deleting a deadline does not delete products: they only lose that due date.
