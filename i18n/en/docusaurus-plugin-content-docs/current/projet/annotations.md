# Annotations

Comment on a specific spot on a plan, image, or PDF, right in the file preview. Each annotation opens a discussion thread shared with the team and, depending on the channel chosen, with project clients.

## Supported files

Annotations are available when you open the preview of an **image** or a **PDF**:

- in the project **Files** tab
- in a product's **Files** tab

Other file types (video, audio, etc.) preview without annotations.

## Create an annotation

1. Open the image or PDF file from **Files**
2. Click the spot on the document you want to comment on (the cursor becomes a crosshair)
3. Choose the discussion **channel**:
   - **External** — visible to the team and clients
   - **Internal** — visible to the team only
4. Optionally **link a product** to provide context
5. Enter your comment
6. Submit to create the annotation

A marker appears at the selected location. On a multipage PDF, the annotation is tied to the **current page**.

:::info
The annotation is only created after you send the first comment. Closing the window without writing anything saves nothing.
:::

## Internal or external channel

Like project chat, each annotation has two channels:

| Channel | Visible to |
|---------|------------|
| **External** | Team and clients |
| **Internal** | Team only |

Switch between them using the tabs at the top of the annotation thread. Clients only see **external** threads on shared files.

:::info
If you link a product **hidden from the client** to an external annotation on a shared file, the annotation does not appear in the client space.
:::

## Link a product

To associate an annotation with a project product:

1. Open or create an annotation
2. Click **Link a product**
3. Search for and select the desired product

A chip with the product thumbnail appears in the thread. Click it to open the product sheet.

## Reply to an annotation

1. Click a marker on the document, or select the annotation in the sidebar panel
2. Enter your reply
3. Send the message

You can **mention** a member or client with `@` to alert them directly.

Each author can edit their own comments after sending.

## Resolve or reopen

When the point has been addressed:

1. Open the annotation
2. Click the resolve icon (checkmark)

The annotation is marked as resolved. You can reopen it at any time with the corresponding icon.

## Show, hide, and navigate

In the preview:

- **View**: show or hide markers on the document
- **Show resolved**: include or exclude resolved annotations from the list and document
- The **Annotations** panel lists all threads for the file; click an entry to open the thread and center the marker
- On a PDF, use page thumbnails to change pages
- Zoom with the `+` / `-` controls to place or read an annotation more precisely

## Delete an annotation

1. Open the annotation
2. Click the delete icon
3. Confirm

Deleting removes the annotation and **all** its comments. This action cannot be undone.

## Notifications

Team members are notified when a comment is added, or when an annotation is resolved or reopened (according to their preferences).

`@` mentions notify the mentioned person, including clients.

To enable or disable these alerts (in-app and email), see [Notifications](/projet/notifications).

## Client side

Clients can annotate files you have made visible. They see **external** markers and threads on those documents.

:::tip
Use annotations to centralize feedback on a plan or technical sheet, rather than multiplying exchanges in the general chat.
:::
