# Annotations

Comment on a specific spot on a plan, image, or PDF, right in the file preview. Each annotation opens a discussion thread shared with the project team and clients.

## Supported files

Annotations are available when you open the preview of an **image** or a **PDF**:

- in the project **Files** tab
- in a product's **Files** tab

Other file types (video, audio, etc.) preview without annotations.

## Create an annotation

1. Open the image or PDF file from **Files**
2. Click the spot on the document you want to comment on (the cursor becomes a crosshair)
3. Enter your comment in the window that opens
4. Submit to create the annotation

A numbered marker appears at the selected location. On a multipage PDF, the annotation is tied to the **current page**.

:::info
The annotation is only created after you send the first comment. Closing the window without writing anything saves nothing.
:::

## Reply to an annotation

1. Click a marker on the document, or select the annotation in the side panel
2. Enter your reply
3. Send the message

You can **mention** a team member or client with `@` to notify them directly.

Each author can edit their own comments after sending.

## Resolve or reopen

When the point is handled:

1. Open the annotation
2. Click the resolve icon (checkmark)

The annotation is marked as resolved. You can reopen it anytime with the matching icon.

## Show, hide, and navigate

In the preview:

- **Show**: shows or hides markers on the document
- **Show resolved**: includes or excludes resolved annotations from the list and the document
- The **Annotations** panel lists all threads on the file; click an entry to open the thread and focus the marker
- On a PDF, use the page thumbnails to change pages
- Zoom with the `+` / `-` controls to place or read an annotation more precisely

## Delete an annotation

1. Open the annotation
2. Click the delete icon
3. Confirm

Deletion removes the annotation and **all** its comments. This action cannot be undone.

## Notifications

Team members are notified when a comment is added, or when an annotation is resolved or reopened (based on their preferences).

`@` mentions notify the mentioned person, including a client.

To enable or disable these alerts (app and email), see [Notifications](/projet/notifications).

## Client side

Clients can annotate files you have made visible. They see the same markers and discussion threads as the team on those documents.

:::tip
Use annotations to centralize feedback on a plan or technical sheet, rather than scattering comments in the general chat.
:::
