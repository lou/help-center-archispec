# Catalogue

The **Catalogue** is your agency's central product library. Products are stored once and reused across all your projects.

:::info
In the Archispec interface, the **Catalogue** menu corresponds to this agency library. Project products are separate and accessible from each project's **Products** tab.
:::

## Access the catalogue

1. In the main menu, click **Catalogue**
2. The list of agency products appears

The catalogue shows **all agency products**, including those from projects you are not a member of. Duplicated products are hidden to avoid duplicates.

## Filter the catalogue

Open the filter bar to refine the list:

| Filter | Usage |
|--------|-------|
| **Project** | Show products from a specific project, or **No project** for catalogue-only products |
| **Type** | Filter by product type |
| **Lot** | Filter by trade |
| **Search** | Search by product name |

Filters combine with each other. Click **Reset** to clear all.

## Add a product to the catalogue

### Create a product manually

1. In **Catalogue**, click **New product**
2. Fill in the information (name, price, images, manufacturer, etc.)
3. Click **Create**

### From the browser extension

1. Navigate to a supplier product page
2. Open the Archispec extension
3. Review the extracted information
4. Leave the **Project** field empty to save the product to the catalogue
5. Click **Add**

See [Using the extension](/extension/utilisation) for details.

## View a catalogue product sheet

Click a product to open its detail view. You'll find:

- Description, images, price, and manufacturer
- Product types and associated lot
- **Linked projects**: clickable chips listing all projects using this product (accessible if you are a member)

## Reuse a product in a project

1. Open a project and go to the **Products** tab
2. Click **Add from library**
3. Filter and check the desired products
4. Click **Add to project**

Products are copied into the project with their information (price, images, descriptions).

## Edit a catalogue product

1. In **Catalogue**, click the product
2. Edit the desired fields
3. Click **Save**

:::info
Editing a catalogue product does not automatically affect copies already added to your projects. Use the **Apply changes to linked products** toggle on a project sheet to sync linked copies — see [Add a product](/produits/ajouter-produit#sync-changes-across-copies).
:::

## Tips

- Build your catalogue as you source products to save time on future projects
- Use the **No project** filter to find products created directly in the catalogue
- Use the extension to quickly populate the catalogue from supplier websites
- Organize your [product types](/agence/types-produits) in agency settings to make filtering easier
