# Companies

**Companies** are your agency's directory of contractor firms (trade companies, installers, etc.). They are assigned as assignees on project [lots](/projet/gestion-lots).

:::info
A company in this directory does **not** have an Archispec account. It is not a team member or a client — it is a reference card used to organize lots.
:::

## Access companies

1. In the main menu, click your agency name
2. Click **Companies**

Only agency administrators and owners can manage this list.

## Add a company

1. Enter the name in the field provided
2. Click **Add**

You can also create a company on the fly when assigning a lot's assignee.

## Rename or delete

- Click the name to edit it inline
- Click the trash icon to delete (after confirmation)
