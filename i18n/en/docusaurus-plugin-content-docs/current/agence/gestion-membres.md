# Member management

Manage your agency members and their permissions.

## Access member management

1. In the main menu, click your agency name
2. Click **Members** in the sidebar
3. All agency members are displayed

## Add a member

1. Click **Invite a new member**
2. Enter the member's email address
3. Choose the role to assign:
   - **Owner**: full access
   - **Administrator**: can manage members and settings
   - **Member**: standard access
4. Choose the projects the member should access
5. Click **Send invitation**
6. The member will receive an email with a link to join the agency


## Roles and permissions

| Action | Owner | Administrator | Member |
|--------|:-----:|:-------------:|:------:|
| Create new projects | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Manage members (add, edit, remove) | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Edit agency settings | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Transfer agency ownership | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |


## Edit a member's permissions

1. Click the dropdown menu to the right of the member
2. Select the new role
3. Changes take effect immediately.


## Manage invitations

### View pending invitations

In the **Members** section, all pending invitations are displayed

### Resend an invitation

If the member did not receive the invitation email:

1. Click "Resend" to the right of the invitation
2. A new email is sent
