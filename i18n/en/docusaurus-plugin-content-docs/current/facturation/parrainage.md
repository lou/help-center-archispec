# Referral program

The referral program lets your agency and the agencies you refer benefit from subscription credits.

## Access referrals

1. In the main menu, click your agency name
2. Go to **Referral**

:::info
Referrals are available to agencies with an active subscription and a configured payment method.
:::

## How it works

1. Share your **referral link** or **code** with another agency
2. The referred agency signs up and subscribes to a paid plan
3. Each party receives **€60 in credit** automatically applied to Stripe billing

| Beneficiary | Credit |
|-------------|--------|
| You (referrer) | €60 |
| Referred agency | €60 |

Credits are applied to upcoming invoices via Stripe.

## Share your code

On the **Referral** page:

1. Copy the **referral link** or **code**
2. Send it to another agency (by email, message, etc.)

The referred agency can also enter your code during sign-up.

## Conditions

- The referrer must have an active subscription with a configured payment method
- The referrer's credit is granted when the referral's first invoice is paid
- An invalid or expired referral code cannot be used

## Common issues

| Message | Likely cause |
|---------|--------------|
| Invalid referral code | Incorrect or expired code |
| Referrer not eligible | The referrer has not yet configured a payment method |

For other billing questions, see [Subscription and limits](/facturation/abonnement-et-limites).
