# Subscription and limits

Archispec offers a free plan and a paid subscription to unlock unlimited product creation and monthly 3D credits.

## Access billing

1. In the main menu, click your agency name
2. Go to **Billing**

Only agency administrators and owners can manage the subscription.

## Free plan

Without a subscription, your agency benefits from:

| Item | Limit |
|------|-------|
| Products | **20 free products** (excluding duplicates) |
| 3D credits | **10 lifetime credits** |
| Projects | Unlimited |
| Clients | Unlimited |
| Exports | Unlimited |
| Members | Unlimited on the free plan |

:::info
Products already created remain accessible and editable even when the limit is reached. Only creating new products (and new projects if the subscription is canceled) is blocked.
:::

When you reach the product limit, a message prompts you to subscribe to continue adding products.

## Paid subscription

### Pricing (excl. tax)

| Plan | Base price | Additional seat |
|------|------------|-----------------|
| **Annual** | €60 / month (billed €720 / year) | €25 / month per seat |
| **Monthly** | €75 / month | €30 / month per seat |

The base price includes **1 user**. Each additional agency member uses one seat.

### Subscription benefits

- **Unlimited** products
- **10 3D credits per month** (renewed each calendar month)
- Seat management via the Stripe portal

## Subscribe

1. Go to **Billing**
2. Choose **Monthly** or **Annual**
3. Adjust the **number of users** with the slider
4. Click **Subscribe**
5. Complete payment on the Stripe page

## Manage subscription

Once subscribed:

1. Go to **Billing**
2. Click **Manage subscription** to open the Stripe portal

From the Stripe portal, you can update your payment method, change the number of seats, or cancel.

:::info
Switching from annual to monthly subscription is only possible at the end of the current period.
:::

## Subscription statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Free** / **Free trial** | Active free plan (20 product limit) |
| **Active** | Paid subscription in progress |
| **Unpaid** | Payment issue — update your bank details |
| **Canceled** | Subscription ended — creation blocked, viewing still possible |

## User seats

Each active member or pending invitation counts toward your subscription seat limit.

If your team is at capacity, you will see a message inviting you to add a seat from the billing portal.

To invite members, see [Member management](/agence/gestion-membres).

## 3D credits

| Plan | Credits |
|------|---------|
| Free | 10 lifetime credits |
| Paid | 10 credits per month |

Each 3D model generation uses **1 credit**. See [Generate a 3D model](/produits/generation-3d) for details.

Remaining credits are visible on the **Billing** page and in the product **3D** tab.
