Archispec glossary

Definitions of terms used in Archispec and this help center.

A​

Agency — Shared workspace grouping members, catalog, and projects.

Annotation — Comment pinned to a specific spot on an image or PDF, with a discussion thread. See Annotations.

Administrator — Intermediate role at agency or project level. Can manage members and settings, but cannot transfer ownership or archive a project.

Member — Base role for team members. Can work on products, files, and chat.

Archiving — Action that removes an item (project or product) from the active list without deleting it. Archiving is reversible.

Progress — Name of the planning tab on the client side (equivalent to Planning on the agency side).

B​

Purchase order — PDF export to order products from a supplier. See Purchase order.

C​

External channel — Chat thread visible to the team and project clients.

Internal channel — Chat thread visible only to the project team.

Spec book — PDF export grouping selected product sheets.

Catalog — Agency's central product library, reusable across all projects. Accessible via the Catalog menu.

Client — External person invited to a project to review proposed products, approve choices, and communicate via chat.

3D credit — Unit consumed to generate a 3D model from a product image (1 credit per generation).

Company — Agency-level contractor firm card, assignable as a lot assignee. See Companies.

F​

Spreadsheet — Excel export of selected products.

L​

Lot — Trade category in a project (plumbing, carpentry, etc.). Products and planning tasks can be linked to a lot. See Managing lots.

M​

Mention — Notification to a member via @ in chat or an annotation. See Chat.

P​

Referral program — Program allowing an agency to refer another agency and receive subscription credits. See Referral program.

Planning — Chronological view (Gantt-style) of project tasks, with dependencies and optional client sharing.

Client portal — Dedicated interface (/client/...) accessible to clients invited to a project. See Overview.

Product — Central element of the specification: furniture, equipment, finish, or fixture. Each product has a status, price, images, and can be linked to an area and lot.

Owner — Highest role. At agency or project level, has all permissions including archiving and ownership transfer.

Proposed — Status indicating a product is presented to the client. Only proposed products (and beyond in the lifecycle) are visible on the client side.

S​

Seat — User slot included in the paid subscription. Each active member or pending invitation uses one seat.

Status — Stage in a product's lifecycle (proposed, liked, approved, ordered, etc.). See Product statuses.

Supply / installation — Product field (when a lot is set) indicating whether the lot covers supply, install, both, or neither.

T​

Technical note — Internal text field on a product, never shown to clients, exportable in the booklet and spreadsheet.

Type — Agency-level product category used for filtering and organizing. See Product types.

Z​

Area — Spatial division of a project (room, floor, space). Used to organize and filter products.
