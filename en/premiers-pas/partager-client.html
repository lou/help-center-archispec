Share with a client

Invite your client to the project so they can review proposed products and communicate with your team.

Invite a client​

  1. Go to project Settings
  2. Click Client in the sidebar
  3. Click Invite a client
  4. Enter the client's email
  5. Click Send invitation

The client receives an email with a link to access their dedicated space.

Check what the client sees​

Before inviting, or after configuring your products:

  1. Use client preview to see the interface as the client will see it
  2. Check that the right products are Proposed (hidden from client products are not visible)

Configure sharing​

In project settings, adjust:

  • Sharing settings: visibility of prices and web links — see Sharing settings
  • Price settings: excl. or incl. tax display — see Price settings

Congratulations​

You have set up your account, agency, first project, and invited a client. Explore the help center to learn more about each feature.
