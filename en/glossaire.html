Archispec glossary

Definitions of terms used in Archispec and in this help center.

A​

Agency — Shared workspace grouping members, catalogue, and projects.

Annotation — Comment anchored to a specific spot on an image or PDF, with an internal or external discussion thread. See Annotations.

Administrator — Intermediate role at agency or project level. Can manage members and settings, but cannot transfer ownership or archive a project.

Associate — Base role for team members. Can work on products, files, and chat.

Archiving — Action that removes an item (project or product) from the active list without deleting it. Archiving is reversible.

Progress — Name of the planning tab on the client side (equivalent to Planning on the agency side).

B​

Purchase order — PDF export to order products from a supplier. See Purchase order.

C​

Catalogue — Agency's central product library, reusable across all projects. See Catalogue.

Client — External person invited to a project to review proposed products, validate choices, and exchange via chat.

External channel — Chat or annotation thread visible to the team and project clients.

3D credit — Unit consumed to generate a 3D model from a product image (1 credit per generation).

D​

Detail booklet — PDF export grouping selected product sheets.

E​

Company — Agency-level record of an intervening company, assignable as a lot contractor. See Companies.

F​

Spreadsheet — Excel export of selected products.

Supply / install — Product field (when a lot is defined) indicating whether the lot handles supply, installation, both, or neither.

H​

Hidden from client — Initial status of a product being prepared. The client does not see it until it is moved to Proposed.

I​

Internal channel — Chat or annotation thread visible to the project team only.

L​

Lot — Trade category in a project (plumbing, carpentry, etc.). Products and planning tasks can be linked to a lot. See Lot management.

M​

Mention — Notification sent to a member via @ in chat or an annotation. See Chat.

N​

Technical note — Internal text field on a product, never visible to clients, exportable in the booklet and spreadsheet.

O​

Owner — Highest role. At agency or project level, has all permissions including archiving and ownership transfer.

P​

Phase — Grouping of planning tasks by project stage (Studies, Construction, etc.). See Planning.

Planning — Chronological view (Gantt-style) of project tasks, with phases, dependencies, and optional client sharing.

Client portal — Dedicated interface (/client/...) accessible to invited clients on a project. See Overview.

Product — Central element of the specification: furniture, equipment, finish, or load. Each product has a status, price, images, and can be linked to a section and lot.

Proposed — Status indicating a product is presented to the client. Only proposed products (and beyond in the lifecycle) are visible on the client side.

Referral — Program allowing an agency to refer another agency and receive subscription credits. See Referral.

S​

Section — Spatial division of a project (room, floor, space). Used to organize and filter products. See Section management.

Seat — User seat included in the paid subscription. Each active member or pending invitation consumes a seat.

Status — Stage in a product's lifecycle (proposed, validated, ordered, etc.). See Product statuses.

T​

Type — Agency-level product category, used to filter and classify. See Product types.
