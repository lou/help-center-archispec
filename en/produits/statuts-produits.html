Product statuses

Each product follows a lifecycle that reflects its progress, from the initial proposal to on-site installation.

Statuses​

StatusDescriptionVisible to client
Hidden from clientProduct being prepared, not yet proposedNo
ProposedProduct presented to the client for reviewYes
RejectedThe client has rejected the productYes
ValidatedThe client has approved the choiceYes
OrderedThe product has been orderedYes
In deliveryThe product has been shippedYes
ReceivedThe product has been received on siteYes
InstalledThe product has been installedYes

Who changes statuses?​

Agency side​

Associates, administrators, and owners can change any product status at any point in the lifecycle.

Client side​

Invited clients can update proposed products:

  • Validate the product
  • Reject the product

Post-validation statuses (ordered, delivery, received, installed) are usually managed by the agency.

Change a product status​

  1. Open the product sheet or use the product list
  2. Click the current status
  3. Select the new status

You can also change the status of multiple products in bulk via Select products.

Best practices​

  • Keep products as Hidden from client until they are ready to be presented
  • Move to Proposed only when the product is complete (images, price, description)
  • Use status filters to track progress — see Filter products
