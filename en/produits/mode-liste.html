List mode

List mode shows products and services as compact rows, with prices always visible, so you can cost and compare quickly.

Enable list mode​

  1. Open your project's Products list
  2. Click the view options (settings) icon in the action bar
  3. Under Layout, choose List

To return to cards, choose Grid.

tip

Your grid / list choice is remembered for later visits.

What list mode shows​

In list mode:

  • each row shows the product or service price
  • the project total (or the selection / active filters total) appears in the action bar
  • each area or lot total appears to the right of the group name

Edit a price from the list​

Click a row's price to open the breakdown (unit price, quantity, markup, taxes) and save without leaving the list. You can also edit the title and status inline on the row.

Totals breakdown​

Click the total in the action bar to open the amount breakdown for:

  • current selection
  • applied filters
  • overall project

For each level: markup, excl. tax, and incl. tax. The services share is shown when present.

Grid mode​

In Grid, cards stay image-focused. Prices are not shown in the agency grid: switch to List to see and edit them.

Image size remains adjustable in the view menu while in grid mode.

On the client side​

In the client portal, the layout stays as a grid. If price sharing is enabled, the client turns on Show prices in the view menu to see amounts on cards and the total in the action bar.
