Products

Manage your products: add, filter, select, export, and generate 3D models.

📄️ Filter products

Use filters to quickly identify relevant products and build your selections.

📄️ Select products

Select one or more products to perform bulk actions.

📄️ Price mode

Price mode is an optional feature that displays a detailed price view in the product list.

📄️ Add a product

Add products to your project from different sources.

📄️ Archive a product

Archiving removes a product from the active list without permanently deleting it.

📄️ Purchase order

Generate a purchase order PDF from your selected products to order from your suppliers.

📄️ Generate a 3D model

Turn a product image into an interactive 3D model using artificial intelligence, then integrate it into your plans.
